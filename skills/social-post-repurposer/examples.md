X posts each under 280 characters, link in the last thread post.

## 2. 150-word note, "only LinkedIn"
Expected: three alternative LinkedIn posts, no padding with invented detail.

## 3. Thai video transcript
Expected: all posts in natural Thai, quotes only from the transcript.
