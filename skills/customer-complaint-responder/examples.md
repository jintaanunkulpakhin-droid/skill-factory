# Test prompts (used by the automatic self-test)

## 1. Late delivery, angry email, policy given ("refund shipping fee if over 7 days late")
Expected: acknowledges the delay specifically, one apology, offers only the
shipping-fee refund, clear next step, 80-180 words, no extra discount.

## 2. 1-star public Google review mentioning rude staff, no policy given
Expected: 40-80 words, no personal details, invites to private contact,
any compensation left as `[offer: ...]`, no blame on the customer.

## 3. Thai Shopee chat threatening to report to consumer protection
Expected: Thai reply, short and neutral, "check with manager" note, no admission of fault.
