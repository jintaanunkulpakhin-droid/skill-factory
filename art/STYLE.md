# Art rules (read before writing any job.json)

## Products
- **clipart**: single objects or small scenes on a plain white background, no
  shadow, centered, room around the edges. Packaged as transparent PNGs.
- **pattern**: an all-over decorative pattern filling the whole frame, no
  border, no single centered object. Packaged as 2048px seamless JPG tiles.
One bundle = one theme, one kind, 12 to 16 prompts x 2 renders (24 to 32 files).

## House style (append to every prompt, per kind)
- clipart: `flat vector illustration, clean bold outlines, soft pastel and gold
  palette, isolated on plain white background, no text, no shadow, centered`
- pattern: `seamless decorative pattern, Thai ornamental motif, evenly spaced,
  flat colors, gold and deep red and teal palette, fills the frame, no text`

## Negative prompt (use as job.json "negative")
`text, letters, watermark, signature, logo, frame, border, photo, realistic,
blurry, extra fingers, deformed hands, cropped, buddha, monk, king, queen,
royal, crown, temple interior, flag`

## Never prompt for
- Buddha images, monks, temple altars or other sacred objects; the royal
  family, royal symbols or the Thai flag; real people, brands or logos.
- A living artist's name or "in the style of" any named artist.
- Any written text (AI garbles Thai script). Text goes on covers only.

## Good subjects
Festivals (krathong, lanterns, Songkran water bowls and flowers), Thai food and
drinks, fruit, flowers (lotus, jasmine garland, orchid, frangipani), elephants
and other animals, tuk-tuks, long-tail boats, floating markets, wooden houses,
kranok and pha sin textile patterns, benjarong ceramic patterns.
